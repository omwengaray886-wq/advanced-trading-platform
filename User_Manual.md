## 2. Core Logic: How the AI Reads the Market
TradeAlgo discards traditional retail indicators (like RSI, MACD, or Bollinger Bands) in favor of raw price action and liquidity engineering. The AI engine is trained to hunt for institutional footprints:

*   **Liquidity Sweeps (BSL/SSL):** The AI identifies where retail traders place their stop-losses (Buy Side / Sell Side Liquidity). Institutions push price into these zones to fill their massive orders before reversing the market.
*   **Fair Value Gaps (FVG) / Imbalances:** When price moves too fast, it leaves a gap in the order book. The AI maps these zones, as price is magnetically drawn back to them to rebalance.
*   **Order Blocks (OB):** The last down-candle before a massive up-move (or vice versa). The AI flags these as institutional footprints where massive capital was injected.
*   **Change of Character (ChoCH) & Break of Structure (BOS):** The AI reads the exact moment a trend shifts from bullish to bearish by analyzing market structure breaks, confirming reversals.

**The "Chain of Verification":** Our AI never guesses. If it claims a trend is bullish, it is required by its internal logic to verify that claim with three specific data points (e.g., price level, liquidity sweep, structural break).

## 4. Step-by-Step: How to Use TradeAlgo

### Step 1: Select Your Asset
Navigate to the **Markets** tab. On the right side, open the **Asset Sidebar**. Click on your desired currency pair (e.g., `BTC/USDT` for crypto, or `XAU/USD` for gold).

### Step 2: Let the AI Scan
Once clicked, you will see a loading sequence. The AI goes through two phases:
1.  **Fast Scan (Structural Markings):** Instantly maps local support/resistance and FVGs.
2.  **Deep Intel (AI Enhancement):** Sends the data to the Gemini 2.0 AI to analyze institutional intent and generate the narrative.

### Step 3: Read the Output
Review the chart for visual markers. Then, read the **Executive Narrative** at the bottom. 
*   Look for the **HTF Bias** (Higher Timeframe Bias).
*   Note the **Entry Context** (where to place your limit order).
*   Check the **Risk Assessment** to see if the market is too volatile to trade.

### Step 4: Manage Your Risk
Never trade blind. Use the platform's built-in **Risk Calculator** in the Signal Lab to ensure you are risking exactly 1% (or your preferred amount) of your capital per trade.
